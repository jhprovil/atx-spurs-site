Upcoming Fixtures

Every match below is listed in Central time. The light tells you whether Tramps is open, closed, or still to be confirmed. If it's amber, check back closer to kickoff or join the email list and we'll tell you.

Green — Tramps is open. Game on.
Amber — not confirmed yet. Check back closer to kickoff.
Red — Tramps is closed for this one.

Kickoff times are converted from the official Tottenham Hotspur fixture list and are provisional. Broadcast selections move matches; the club confirms changes a few weeks out. Cup rounds show as dates only until the draw is made.

How the light gets set

Amber Isn't A Maybe Forever

Most matches turn green a week out, once we've confirmed screens and staffing with Tramps. Nine in the morning on a Saturday is easy. A Wednesday two o'clock in December takes a little more arranging.

If a match matters to you and it's still amber, say so. A handful of people asking is usually all it takes to get the doors open.
