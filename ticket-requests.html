Before you ask

What You Need

1

To Be One Of Us

There's no formal membership to sign up for. Coming to a match at Tramps and/or owning some Austin Spurs gear is enough.

2

A Paid Tottenham Membership

Bought from Tottenham directly, which gets you a Client Reference Number. You need one CRN for every ticket you're asking for, children included.

Before you get invested

Good To Know

No Guarantees

We aren't promised tickets. We've been turned down outright, and some matches come back short of what we asked for.

Kickoff Times Move

Broadcast picks can shift a weekend match to Friday or Monday night. Wait for the confirmed time before booking travel.

Claim What You Win

Log into your Tottenham account and buy the ticket. Anything left unclaimed is resold on the next public sale date.

If You're Turned Down

Try a One Hotspur sale date, the official exchange, or Hospitality. Steer clear of third-party resellers.

If You Can't Go

Tell us. We're in a network of supporters club chairs and can usually find someone to take it.

For families

Junior Flag Bearers

Junior members of official supporters clubs can serve as flag bearers. That means on the pitch an hour before kickoff for warmups, then out on the field holding a flag during the pre-match handshakes. Three of our junior members did it two seasons ago.
