Why not the real thing

Tottenham's face is a custom family F37 Foundry built for the club — proprietary, all rights reserved, no licence available at any price. F37's own notes say the display cut was sharpened to echo the cockerel's beak and spur, with joins cut and small serifs added. So the target is that quality: condensed caps with angular, cut terminals. Every option below is a cousin, none is a copy.
